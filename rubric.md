# Fitment rubric

The shared scoring contract for `score` and `tailor`. Edit this for your own
search — the skills read it verbatim, so changing the rubric here changes how
every role is scored without touching any skill.

> Replace everything in `<angle brackets>` with your own criteria. The example
> values below are written for a senior Technical Program Manager search; swap
> them for whatever role you're targeting.

---

## Must-haves (hard gates)

A role that misses any of these scores **2 or lower**, regardless of how good
the rest looks.

1. **Title fit** — the title is `<your target role or an accepted equivalent>`
   (e.g. Technical Program Manager, Senior Program Manager, Technical Project
   Manager). A bare/mismatched title is a miss.
2. **Company type** — `<the kind of employer you want>` (e.g. a product or
   engineering-led company, not a consulting/staffing/advisory firm).
3. **Posting is live** — fetched successfully, posted recently (within
   `<N>` days), and not marked closed/filled.
4. **Location / work authorization** — `<your location rule>` (e.g. remote in
   your country, or onsite/hybrid in an acceptable city). Treat work
   authorization as a hard blocker; treat mere geographic *preference* as a
   soft signal (see below).

## Strong-positive signals (add to the score)

- Domain overlap with your background (`<your domains>`).
- Scope match — headcount, budget, or cross-team breadth in your range.
- Technical-fluency signals you can speak to (`<your tech>`).
- Salary at or above your floor (`<your number + currency>`).
- A brand or mission you'd genuinely want to work for.

## Anti-signals (subtract from the score)

- `<domains you're not ready to interview for>` as the *central* requirement
  (e.g. an AI/ML-centered role if that's not your depth yet).
- A title that looks adjacent but is really `<coordinator / delivery / status-
  meeting>` work.
- `<employer types you're avoiding>`.
- Hardware-only scope when you want software, or vice versa.

## Location as a soft signal (single-role scoring only)

When scoring one role you're already curious about, don't auto-zero on
geography — grade honestly and flag it:

- Preferred city / country-wide remote → no penalty.
- Remote restricted to regions you can't work from → −1, and flag it.
- Onsite in a relocation city with no remote option → −2, and call out the move.
- No work authorization in the posted location → hard miss (see must-have #4).

## Score → verdict

| Score | Meaning | Worth applying? |
|-------|---------|-----------------|
| 5 | Strong match on must-haves + several positives | Yes |
| 4 | Solid match, minor gaps | Yes |
| 3 | Mixed — real gaps or notable anti-signals | No (unless you have a reason) |
| 2 | Misses a must-have | No |
| 1 | Wrong role or wrong company type | No |
